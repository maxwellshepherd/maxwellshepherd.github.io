Nothing here is pre-composed. Every phrase is assembled while you listen from small musical primitives: a grammar of section roles, a weighted chord graph over six modes, a motif that is repeated, sequenced, inverted and fragmented, ostinato cells with Glass-style accent groupings, and an arranger that decides who plays. Each of those choices is a roll of weighted dice, shown on the right at the moment you hear it.

Orchestral samples: VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0). Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0). Inspired by Mychael Danna, Philip Glass and Hans Zimmer.

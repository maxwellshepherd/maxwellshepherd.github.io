Form

Nothing here is pre-composed. Each piece begins by planning its own arc, choosing where its climax will fall, and by building an eight-bar theme from a short motif. The theme is stated, taken apart in the sections that follow, and brought back by the full orchestra at the climax. Every phrase is assembled while you listen from small musical primitives: phrases that pair up as question and answer, a weighted chord graph over six modes with borrowed and chromatic colours, ostinato cells with Glass-style accent groupings, composed transitions between sections, and an arranger that brings voices in one at a time. The tempo breathes at the ends of phrases, held chords swell, and the orchestra accents the strong bars. Each of those choices is a weighted random pick, shown alongside the music at the moment you hear it.

Orchestral samples: VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0). Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0). Inspired by Mychael Danna, Philip Glass and Hans Zimmer.

The composer's choices

Each card is a moment where the composer picked between options at random, weighted toward what suits the music at that point. Cards appear as the result becomes audible.
